Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Color Memory is a mobile application that allows users to capture colors using the device camera, store them with numeric values, organize them into categories, and search saved colors. The app also displays advertisements to support development.

2. Information We Collect

Camera Data:

  • The app uses the camera only to detect colors in real time.
  • No images or videos are stored or uploaded.

Stored Data:

  • Color values (RGB / HEX / numeric codes)
  • User-defined categories
  • Saved color history (stored locally only)

Advertising Data (AdMob):

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information (model, OS)
  • Approximate location
  • Ad interactions

3. How We Use Data

  • Store and organize colors locally
  • Enable search and retrieval of colors
  • Display advertisements via Google AdMob

4. Data Storage

  • All user data is stored locally on the device
  • No data is uploaded to external servers
  • Data is deleted when the app is uninstalled

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob for advertisements.
Google Privacy Policy

6. Permissions

  • Camera permission → for detecting colors
  • Internet permission → for loading ads

7. Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years old.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Some limited data may be shared automatically with Google AdMob.
