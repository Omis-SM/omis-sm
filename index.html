Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Color Memory is a mobile application that allows users to capture colors using the device camera, store them with numeric values, organize them into categories, and search saved colors. The app also displays advertisements to support development using Google AdMob and Unity Ads.

2. Information We Collect

Camera Data:

  • The app uses the camera only to detect colors in real time.
  • No images or videos are stored or uploaded.

Stored Data:

  • Color values (RGB / HEX / numeric codes)
  • User-defined categories
  • Saved color history (stored locally only)

Advertising Data (AdMob & Unity Ads):

  • Device or other IDs (including Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, views)
  • App activity and performance data

3. How We Use Data

  • Store and organize colors locally on your device
  • Enable search and retrieval of saved colors
  • Analytics: Understand how users interact with the app and improve performance
  • Advertising & Marketing: Display relevant advertisements via AdMob and Unity Ads
  • Measure ad performance and optimize ad delivery

4. Data Storage

  • All user-created data (colors, categories, history) is stored locally on the device
  • No user data is uploaded to external servers by the app developer
  • Advertising data is collected and processed by AdMob and Unity Ads according to their privacy policies
  • Local data is deleted when the app is uninstalled

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party advertising services:

  • Google AdMob – for displaying advertisements.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads – for displaying advertisements.
    Unity Privacy Policy

These third-party services may collect and process data as described in their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to any third party.
  • Data is shared automatically with AdMob and Unity Ads for the purpose of displaying advertisements and analytics.
  • Some limited data (such as Advertising ID and approximate location) may be transferred to third-party ad partners.
  • Data collection is optional – users can limit ad personalization through device settings.

7. Permissions

  • Camera permission – for detecting and capturing colors
  • Internet permission – for loading advertisements

8. Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years old. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Your Choices and Controls

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your device's Google Settings > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID at any time from your device settings.
  • You may uninstall the app at any time to delete all locally stored data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
