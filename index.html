Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Color Memory and Pixel Tone are mobile applications published by Learnova Apps. Color Memory allows users to capture colors using the device camera, store them with numeric values, organize them into categories, and search saved colors. Both apps display advertisements to support development and do not collect, store, or share any personal data beyond what is required for ad functionality.

2. Information We Collect

Camera Data:

  • The app uses the camera only to detect colors in real time.
  • No images or videos are stored or uploaded.

Stored Data:

  • Color values (RGB / HEX / numeric codes)
  • User-defined categories
  • Saved color history (stored locally only)

Advertising Data (AdMob):

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information (model, OS)
  • Approximate location
  • Ad interactions

3. How We Use Data

  • Store and organize colors locally
  • Enable search and retrieval of colors
  • Display advertisements via Google AdMob

4. Data Storage

  • All user data is stored locally on the device
  • No data is uploaded to external servers
  • Data is deleted when the app is uninstalled

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob for advertisements.
Google Privacy Policy

6. Consent Sharing Between Apps

If you use both Color Memory and Pixel Tone, your privacy consent choices (such as accepting or rejecting personalised ads) will be shared between both apps. This allows you to make your choice once and have it apply across both applications. Both apps are published by Learnova Apps and follow the same privacy practices described in this policy.

7. Permissions

  • Camera permission → for detecting colors
  • Internet permission → for loading ads

8. Children's Privacy

These apps are intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Some limited data may be shared automatically with Google AdMob. No data is shared with any other third parties.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
